## Blog

### What is Umrah?
In order to fulfill their spiritual obligation, many Muslims from Bangladesh and other countries come to Saudi Arabia each year to do the Umrah. This is in contrast to Hajj, an annual pilgrimage to Makkah that must be completed within the first ten days of the Islamic month of Dhul Hijjah. Umrah, which means "a visit" to the Holy Kaaba in Saudi Arabia, can be done by anybody at any time of year. All Muslims engage in the Umrah, an amazing spiritual journey, to recognize Allah as the creator of peace and to interact with him in order to purify their souls of their impurities. Our Umrah package is arranged with care, transparency, and reliability. Whether you seek a standard, economy, or custom package, Antarik Hajj Kafela is here to make your pilgrimage smooth, comfortable, and spiritually rewarding.

### How to perform Umrah?
There are several spiritual and material advantages to performing Umrah as a traveler. It is thought that performing Umrah atones for one's sins and wins Allah's favor. According to the Prophet Muhammad (PBUH), the reward for undertaking Umrah is equivalent to Jihad, or striving in Allah's way.

**Steps to perform Umrah:**
1. **Niyyah (intention):** before leaving your home or on reaching the designated Miqat, make the sincere intention to perform Umrah solely for the sake of Allah. Say the Talbiyah: *Labbayk Allahumma Umrah* (Here I am, O Allah, for Umrah).
2. **Ihram:** wear Ihram garments (for men, two white unstitched sheets; for women, a modest dress covering the body). Pray two rak'ahs (optional Sunnah) before entering Ihram. Avoid forbidden acts during Ihram (cutting nails or hair, using perfume, marital relations, hunting, etc.).
3. **Recite the Talbiyah:** *Labbayk Allahumma Labbayk, Labbayka Laa Shareeka Laka Labbayk. Innal-Hamda wan-Ni'mata Laka wal-Mulk, Laa Shareeka Lak.* (Here I am, O Allah, here I am. Here I am. You have no partner; here I am. Indeed, all praise and blessings are Yours, and sovereignty too. You have no partner.)
4. **Perform Tawaf:** enter Masjid al-Haram with your right foot and say the dua for entering the mosque. Begin from the Black Stone (Hajar al-Aswad): raise your hand towards it and say "Bismillah, Allahu Akbar." Go around the Kaaba seven times. Men should do Raml (brisk walking) in the first three rounds, if possible. After Tawaf, pray two rak'ahs behind Maqam Ibrahim, if possible.
5. **Perform Sa'i between Safa and Marwah:** start at Safa, face the Kaaba, raise your hands and make dua. Walk towards Marwah making dua and dhikr. Men run between the green lights in each lap. Complete seven trips (Safa → Marwah = 1 lap).
6. **Taqsir:** men shave the head completely (preferred) or trim the hair evenly. Women trim a small portion (about an inch) from the ends of their hair.
7. **End of Umrah:** visit Madinah and pray in the Prophet's Mosque (Masjid an-Nabawi). Pay your respects at the grave of the Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him). During the last 10 days of Ramadan, perform I'tikaf at the Grand Mosque in Makkah or another mosque.
